The probe workspace holds the task as delivered -- the raw database, raw_data/,
docs/data_contract.yaml, config/layer_dependencies.yaml -- and not the Worker's
SQL or the database it builds. What can be checked here is therefore what the
raw source can settle.

The contract carries the parts worth checking that way. Its validation_rules and
their on_failure, its row_filters, its not_null and unique constraints and the
declared grain are all claims about the source, so a figure the Worker reports
for any of them can be recomputed. Its descriptions and business_logic are prose
and cannot.
